## November 16 · Erhai’s east shore {#nov-16}

**Route:** Homestay → Shuanglang Ancient Town → Wase Pier → Xiaoputuo → Wenbi Village → Xiangyang Village → homestay.

Travel north to south along Erhai’s east shore. Use a car between stops. Keeping the same car for the day reduces time spent finding rides, though you can also hail separate rides for each leg.

| Time | Place | Plan | Transport |
|---|---|---|---|
| 08:30–09:30 | Homestay | Breakfast | — |
| 09:30–10:40 | To Shuanglang | Leave Xiangyang Village for the public parking area in Shuanglang Ancient Town | Car, about 45–70 minutes |
| 10:40–12:15 | Shuanglang Ancient Town | Walk from the town’s lanes toward Yuji Bridge, then explore the lakeside path, shops, and Bai residential quarter | Walk |
| 12:15–13:20 | Shuanglang | Lunch and a break | Walk |
| 13:20–14:15 | Wase Pier | Follow East Erhai Ring Road to Wase and visit the public area around the pier | Car, about 25–40 minutes |
| 14:15–15:25 | Haiyin Village, Xiaoputuo | Go to Haiyin Village across from Xiaoputuo and visit the lakeshore around the island | Car, about 10–20 minutes, then walk |
| 15:25–18:30 | Wenbi Village | Follow East Erhai Ring Road to Wenbi Village; explore its lanes and lakeside road, and stay until dusk | Car, about 15–30 minutes, then walk |
| 18:30–20:00 | Xiangyang Village, homestay | Return to Xiangyang Village, have dinner, and go back to the homestay | Car, about 10–25 minutes |

### Stops and food along the way

**Shuanglang Ancient Town:** Walk in from the public parking area and explore Yuji Bridge and the lakeside lanes. Public areas of the old town are free. For lunch, try **Shuanglang Cultural Courtyard Lakeview Restaurant (双廊文化大院海景餐厅)** near Yuji Bridge. It serves Bai-style hot-and-sour fish, vegetables, and Yunnan home cooking.

**Wase Pier:** Visit the public area around the pier for about 30–45 minutes, then continue along the shore to Haiyin Village.

**Xiaoputuo:** A small island in the lake near Haiyin Village. This itinerary visits the public walking area on shore; no boat is needed.

**Wenbi Village:** The village has public viewpoints, cafés, and places to rest. Have dinner here in the evening or return to Xiangyang Village for dinner.

**Dinner:** In Xiangyang Village, try the copper-pot beef hot pot at **Minjiayuan (民家苑)**, No. 1, Group 6, Xiangyang Village, or have Bai home cooking near the homestay.

### Transport and admission

- Public areas of Shuanglang Ancient Town, Wase Pier, the shore across from Xiaoputuo, and Wenbi Village are planned as **free to visit**.
- Some sections of G348 on East Erhai Ring Road are under road maintenance, and traffic may be released in alternating directions. If there is a long delay, shorten the Wase Pier stop while keeping Shuanglang, Xiaoputuo, and Wenbi Village in the itinerary.
- Drive times are planning estimates; follow live navigation on the day.

| Time | Place | Plan | Transport |

| 08:30–09:30 | Homestay | Breakfast | — |
| 09:30–10:50 | To Dali Ancient Town | Leave Xiangyang Village and go around the south end of Erhai to the area near the town’s South Gate | Ride-hailing car, about 60–85 minutes |
| 10:50–12:30 | Dali Ancient Town | Walk around the South Gate, Fuxing Road, Wuhua Tower, and the main streets | Walk |
| 12:30–13:40 | Dali Ancient Town | Lunch and free time around Renmin Road | Walk |

| 17:15–18:00 | Back to the old town | Return to Renmin Road or the South Gate area | Ride-hailing car, about 20–40 minutes |

### Sights and meals

**Dali Ancient Town:** Enter through the South Gate and walk along Fuxing Road, past Wuhua Tower, and along Renmin Road. The main streets and public lanes are free. The old town has Bai architecture, handicraft shops, local snacks, and restaurants.

**Longkan Pier:** On the edge of the Erhai Ecological Corridor on the lake’s west shore. Walk out and back on the public lakeside paths; adjust the distance to suit your schedule. Motor vehicles are restricted inside the corridor, so arrange the ride-hailing drop-off on the perimeter road.

**Lunch and dinner:** In the old town, try cold chicken rice noodles, ersi rice noodles, rushan milk curd, or steam-pot chicken. **Zai Huishou Chicken Rice Noodles (再回首鸡肉米线, Renmin Road Branch 1)** on Renmin Road is an option for lunch or dinner; nearby Yunnan restaurants are another choice.

### Transport and admission

- Public streets in Dali Ancient Town and the public walking areas around Longkan Pier and the Erhai Ecological Corridor are free.
- Dali Ancient Town and Longkan Pier are relatively close; a ride-hailing car is convenient between them. Follow on-site rules for pedestrian areas and the ecological corridor.
- If traffic on the west shore is slow, shorten the afternoon stop at Longkan Pier and keep the time to return to the old town in the evening.

## November 18 · Cangshan Mountains {#nov-18}

**Route:** Homestay → Ximatan Ropeway lower station → Qilongnü Pool → Zhonghe Ropeway → Gantong Ropeway → Qingbi Stream → Jizhao Nunnery → homestay.

Travel by car between the lower stations of the different ropeways. Take the Zhonghe Ropeway up in the morning and the Gantong Ropeway up in the afternoon, then visit Jizhao Nunnery after descending. Each ropeway has a separate entrance; take the same ropeway back down to its own station.

| 08:50–10:10 | To Ximatan Ropeway lower station | Travel from Haidong to the ropeway entrance beside Tianlong Babu Film Studio | Ride-hailing car, about 70–85 minutes |
| 10:10–11:20 | Ximatan Ropeway lower section, Qilongnü Pool | Take Ximatan Ropeway No. 1 up and down; visit the open area at Qilongnü Pool station | Round-trip ropeway, walk |
| 11:20–11:45 | To Zhonghe Ropeway | Go to the Zhonghe Ropeway lower station near March Street | Ride-hailing car, about 15–25 minutes |
| 11:45–12:55 | Zhonghe Ropeway, Zhonghe Temple | Take the ropeway up and down, and walk around near Zhonghe Temple | Round-trip ropeway, walk |
| 12:55–13:30 | To Gantong Ropeway | Go to the Gantong Ropeway lower station near Gantong Temple; have a simple lunch en route or in the car | Ride-hailing car, about 20–35 minutes |
| 13:30–15:15 | Gantong Ropeway, Qingbi Stream | Ride the Gantong Ropeway up and down; visit the open area near Qingbi Stream | Round-trip ropeway, walk |

This schedule assumes smooth connections. **If queues are long at the Ximatan lower section or Zhonghe Ropeway, adjust time at each station to match actual departures and the last uphill service.** Ropeway operations and mountain paths are subject to the scenic area’s notices on the day.

### Ropeways and locations

**Ximatan Ropeway lower section and Qilongnü Pool:** The lower station is near Tianlong Babu Film Studio. Take Ropeway No. 1 to the intermediate station at Qilongnü Pool, visit the open area, then descend on the same ropeway.

**Zhonghe Ropeway:** The lower station is near March Street and uses open chairlifts. The upper station is near Zhonghe Temple. Walk within the open area after arriving, then take the ropeway back down. A one-way ride may take about 20–25 minutes, so allow enough time for the morning visit.

**Gantong Ropeway and Qingbi Stream:** The lower station is near Gantong Temple, and the upper station leads to the Qingbi Stream scenic area. Follow the scenic area’s paths and open-area boundaries, then descend by ropeway.

**Jizhao Nunnery:** On the hillside in the Gantong Temple area. From near the Gantong Ropeway lower station, the uphill walk on open paths usually takes about 20–35 minutes. Visitors can see the courtyards and planted areas. Vegetarian meals are generally served around midday; plan for an ordinary visit in the afternoon.

Prices vary depending on whether the ropeway ticket is bundled with scenic area admission. Check the day’s ticket options at the ticket window or official mini-program. If taking multiple ropeways in one day, ask at the window how scenic area admission is handled.

**Ximatan operating notice:** From **November 9–30, 2026**, Ximatan Ropeway **No. 2 (upper section)** is suspended for maintenance; **No. 1 (lower section to Qilongnü Pool)** is operating normally. **Trail notice:** The Qingbi Stream hiking trail was temporarily closed in April 2026; the scenic area later announced that it would reopen on **May 12**. Follow on-site signs and management instructions on November 18.

**Weather and transport:** Temperatures on Cangshan’s slopes are lower than by Erhai. Zhonghe Ropeway uses open chairlifts. In strong wind, rain, or icy conditions, adjust the order of visits according to ropeway suspension and trail restriction notices. Use cars to transfer between entrances; the upper stations of different ropeways are not connected by road.

### Luoquan Peninsula and lunch

**Luoquan Peninsula:** On Erhai’s east shore. The scenic area includes Tianjing Pavilion, Luoquan Tower, and lakeside paths. Start at Tianjing Pavilion and walk toward Luoquan Tower. The publicly listed reference price for a standard ticket starts at **¥28/person**; climbing the tower may cost extra. Confirm the day’s prices at the scenic area ticket window or through an official channel.

**Lunch in Xiangyang Village:** Have copper-pot beef at Minjiayuan or choose local home cooking, ersi rice noodles, or similar dishes near the homestay. Keep the afternoon within Xiangyang Village on Erhai’s east shore so it is easy to return to the homestay for luggage.

### Airport transfer

- At **17:30**, leave the homestay for Dali Fengyi Airport; set the destination to “Dali Fengyi Airport Domestic Departures” (大理凤仪机场国内出发). The publicly listed route from the homestay is about 19 km, usually a 30–50 minute drive.
- Aim to arrive at the airport between **18:00 and 18:20**. Check in and drop off luggage first, then go through security.
- The flight is scheduled to depart at **20:35** and arrive at Shanghai Pudong T2 at **00:05 on November 20**. Check-in and boarding deadlines follow Spring Airlines’ notices and the boarding pass.
- After arriving at Pudong Airport around midnight, choose a taxi or overnight airport transport based on the actual arrival time.
