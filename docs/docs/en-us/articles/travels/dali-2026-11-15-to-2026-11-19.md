## November 16 · Erhai’s east shore {#nov-16}

Take a private car to Shuanglang, the northernmost stop, then follow East Erhai Ring Road south toward the homestay. The plan uses public paths and shoreline areas, with **¥0 in admission**. Driving times are planning estimates and allow for pickup and drop-off at village entrances.

- **09:00–10:35** Have breakfast at the homestay and leave at 09:30. Allow 45–65 minutes by car. Ask the driver to drop you in the public parking area in Shuanglang.
- **10:35–12:15** Walk through Shuanglang Ancient Town (双廊古镇) and follow the public lakeside path to the outside of Yuji Bridge (玉几桥). Photograph Bai rooflines, fishing boats, and Cangshan across the water. The route stays on land and the town’s public areas are free.
- **12:15–13:30** Have lunch at Shuanglang Cultural Courtyard Lakeview Restaurant (双廊文化大院海景餐厅) by Yuji Bridge. Order Bai-style hot-and-sour fish and seasonal vegetables. The listing gives 11:00–22:00 and an average spend of about ¥69 per person. Budget ¥60–100. [Restaurant listing](https://gs.ctrip.com/html5/you/foods/fooddetail/28/141871.html)
- **13:30–16:00** Drive 25–40 minutes to Wase Pier (挖色码头), then photograph the open water from 14:10 to 15:00. Drive another 10–15 minutes to Haiyin Village (海印村). From 15:20 to 16:00, photograph the temple island and waterbirds from behind the public railings across from Xiaoputuo (小普陀).
- **16:00–18:35** Drive 15–25 minutes to Wenbi Village (文笔村). For a break, try Sunset Mountain and Lake Café (日落·山海咖啡), where optional refreshments are budgeted at ¥40–60. From 17:00, look west from the village’s public walking area to photograph the water, layered ridges, and fading light. If Cangshan blocks the direct sun early, wait until about 18:30 for the mountain silhouette and afterglow. Use the public pedestrian areas. [Café listing](https://gs.ctrip.com/html5/you/foods/fooddetail/1445616/144301825.html)
- **18:35–20:00** Drive 10–20 minutes back to Xiangyang Village. At 19:00, try beef hot pot served in a traditional copper pot at Minjiayuan (民家苑), 1, Group 6, Xiangyang Village. Evening hours for this date could not be confirmed. If closed, ask the homestay front desk whether its restaurant can serve dinner. Arrive at the homestay around 20:00. [Minjiayuan on Amap](https://www.amap.com/place/B0FFLJWH2X)

In strong crosswinds, low cloud, or showers, shorten the Wase stop and linger over lunch in Shuanglang or wait indoors for better light in Wenbi. Maintenance on G348 East Erhai Ring Road and S226 on the west shore overlaps this trip. Affected sections may alternate traffic or close intermittently. Roadside work also begins in Shuanglang on November 16. If delayed, skip Wase Pier first and keep Xiaoputuo and Wenbi. Follow the driver’s live navigation. ## November 17 · Xizhou and Erhai’s west shore {#nov-17}

Go to Xizhou after breakfast and spend a leisurely afternoon in Panxi. Have the driver compare live journey times via Shangguan on the north side and around the south end of the lake. Return to the east shore from Panxi via the south end. Walk inside the Erhai Ecological Corridor and meet the driver outside.

- **09:00–11:15** Have breakfast at the homestay and leave for Xizhou (喜洲) at 09:30. Allow 80–105 minutes. Get out near Zhengyi Gate (正义门).
- **11:15–12:30** Walk from Zhengyi Gate to the Corner Building (转角楼), Sifang Street (四方街), and the public paths beside the fields. Photograph Bai gateways, screen walls, lanes, and fields beneath Cangshan. The appearance of the fields in November varies. Public areas of the old town are free. Try a flaky layered flatbread at Hanlin Posubaba (翰林破酥粑粑), or buy one fresh from a stall on Sifang Street if the shop is closed.
- **12:30–13:45** Have lunch at Fuchun Restaurant (喜洲富椿餐厅, Sifang Street). Try cold chicken rice noodles, rushan milk-curd soup, or steam-pot chicken. Budget ¥50–90 and check the day’s opening hours and menu. [Fuchun menu reference](https://you.ctrip.com/food/dalicity1445616/149810423-dianping.html)
- **13:45–16:50** Drive 25–40 minutes to the parking area outside Panxi S Bend (磻溪 S 湾). Walk out and back along the free lakeside path from around 14:25 to 16:50. Use a telephoto lens for the curve, waterbirds, the distant shore, and slanting afternoon light, or take a break out of the wind. Bicycle hire is optional. Budget ¥0–45, depending on the shop’s quote.
- **16:50–20:00** Drive 75–100 minutes back to Xiangyang Village via Erhai’s south end. Have dinner around 19:00. Ask the homestay to arrange hot ersi rice noodles or Bai home cooking, or eat at a restaurant in Xiangyang Village. Arrive at the homestay around 20:00.

In rain, spend more time in Xizhou’s courtyards and cafés in place of Panxi. Confirm admission prices and opening hours before entering. If traffic builds up, leave Panxi around 16:00 and have dinner nearby. [Xizhou Bai architecture, Dali City](https://www.yndali.gov.cn/dlsrmzf/c106679/pc/content/1983711388713979904/content_1983711388713979904.html) · [West-shore Ecological Corridor access](https://www.yndali.gov.cn/dlsrmzf/c106724/pc/content/1983764619263578112/content_1983764619263578112.html)

## November 18 · The Cangshan Mountains, Dali {#nov-18}

**Take the Gantong Ropeway round trip.** The lower station is near Gantong Temple at about 2,261 m. The upper station is in the Qingbi Stream Scenic Area at about 2,552 m. It suits a short, easy walk on the mountainside, and the gondola gives views of forested slopes and Erhai with a modest elevation gain. Ximatan Lake is about 3,966 m above sea level. The city’s Dali Fabu account relayed the operator’s notice that **Ximatan’s No. 2 upper section is scheduled to close for maintenance November 9–30**. The No. 1 lower section to Qilongnü Pool is scheduled to operate. A ropeway ride to the top could not be verified for this date. [Official Gantong Ropeway information](https://www.yndali.gov.cn/dlsrmzf/c106727/1983766502048894976/PIid6PJf.pdf) · [Ximatan closure notice](https://m.weibo.cn/2/detail/5363294820631250) · [Ximatan route](https://www.dalitravel.cn/content/read/167.html)

- **09:00–11:00** Have breakfast at the homestay and pack a portable lunch. Leave at 09:30, drive around the south end of Erhai to the **Gantong Ropeway lower station (感通索道下站)**, and allow 60–85 minutes. Ask about operating status on November 17, then check wind, ticketing, and trail access with the operator at breakfast on November 18. Enter around 11:00.
- **11:00–13:00** From the upper station, follow the marked open paths to Qingbi Stream (清碧溪) and the viewpoints near the Zhenlong Chessboard (珍珑棋局). Take a short 20–30-minute walk along the open southern section of Jade Belt Trail (玉带路), then return to the same upper station for the ride down at about 13:00. Photograph the layers of the ravine and drifting cloud. On the gondola, move the lens close to the glass to reduce reflections. Skip closed boardwalks, icy paths, and unopened mountain trails. [Qingbi Stream trail notice](https://www.dlzrm.cn/daliwenl/2026/0510/73925.html)
- **13:00–15:05** Eat your packed lunch at the lower station, then follow the designated foothill paths to Gantong Temple (感通寺) and Jizhao Nunnery (寂照庵). Allow 20–35 minutes uphill to the nunnery. Public listings put the nunnery’s vegetarian meal at about 11:30 and ¥20. This itinerary includes a packed lunch. Photograph the plants and eaves, and follow posted photography rules inside. [Jizhao Nunnery vegetarian meal reference](https://travelnews.qunar.com/qa_strategy/dali-jizhaoan-travel-guide)
- **15:05–19:45** Drive 20–30 minutes to Dali Ancient Town (大理古城). Walk from South Gate to Fuxing Road, past Wuhua Tower (五华楼), and along Renmin Road. Public streets are free. Have dinner around 17:45 at Zai Huishou Chicken Rice Noodles (再回首鸡肉米线, Renmin Road Branch 1, No. 198). Try cold chicken rice noodles or shredded chicken rice rolls. Add a grilled rushan milk-curd snack if you like. The restaurant listing gives approximate hours of 09:30–20:00. After dinner, photograph the rooflines and streetlights along Fuxing Road and outside Wuhua Tower as night falls. [Restaurant listing](https://tw.trip.com/restaurant/china/dali-city/detail/zai-hui-shou-183639/)
- **19:45–21:10** Allow 60–85 minutes by car back to the homestay. If live navigation predicts arrival at or after 22:00, leave the ancient town earlier.
